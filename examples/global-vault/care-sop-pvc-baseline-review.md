approved_steps:
  - step_id: care.pvc.identify-and-inspect
    instructions:
      en: Confirm the exact model and that the applicable part is PVC or soft vinyl. Stop for cracks, hardening, swelling, sticky surfaces, coating loss, exposed structure, or electronic components.
      zh: 确认准确型号，并确认对应部件为 PVC 或软质乙烯材料。发现开裂、硬化、膨胀、表面粘腻、涂层脱落、结构外露或电子部件时停止处理。
  - step_id: care.pvc.power-and-accessories
    instructions:
      en: Power off the product, disconnect only user-removable power parts, and remove clothing, wigs, and accessories that may transfer dye or retain moisture.
      zh: 关闭产品电源，仅断开用户可拆卸的供电部件，并移除可能迁移染料或积聚水分的衣物、假发和附件。
  - step_id: care.pvc.surface-clean
    instructions:
      en: Use a soft non-abrasive cloth with lukewarm water and only the mild cleaner approved for the model. Wipe gently without solvents, hard scrubbing, high heat, or unapproved soaking.
      zh: 使用柔软无研磨性的布、温水以及该型号明确允许的温和清洁剂轻柔擦拭，不使用溶剂、不用力摩擦、不使用高温，也不进行未经批准的浸泡。
  - step_id: care.pvc.detail-and-cavity-clean
    instructions:
      en: Protect painted details, seams, adhesives, and non-waterproof components. Clean cavities only with the model-approved tool and avoid forcing water into internal structures.
      zh: 保护涂色细节、接缝、粘接区域和非防水部件。内部空腔只能使用该型号认可的工具清洁，并避免强行让水进入内部结构。
  - step_id: care.pvc.remove-residue
    instructions:
      en: Remove cleaner residue within the model's approved water-exposure limits and do not leave detergent trapped in folds, cavities, seams, or connectors.
      zh: 在该型号允许的进水范围内清除清洁剂残留，不让清洁剂滞留在褶皱、空腔、接缝或接口中。
  - step_id: care.pvc.dry
    instructions:
      en: Blot with a clean lint-free cloth and air-dry fully in a shaded, ventilated location. Do not use sunlight, heaters, hot air, or other direct heat.
      zh: 使用干净无绒软布轻压吸水，并在阴凉通风处彻底自然干燥。不得使用阳光、暖气、热风或其他直接热源。
  - step_id: care.pvc.surface-treatment
    instructions:
      en: Do not apply oil, plasticizer, polish, powder, or restoration chemicals unless the exact PVC formulation and treatment are approved by the supplier.
      zh: 除非供应商针对该 PVC 配方和处理方法明确批准，否则不要使用油剂、增塑剂、抛光剂、粉剂或修复化学品。
  - step_id: care.pvc.lubricant
    instructions:
      en: Use only a supplier-approved water-based lubricant and stop if the PVC becomes sticky, swollen, hardened, discolored, or develops an unusual odor.
      zh: 仅使用供应商批准的水性润滑剂。若 PVC 出现粘腻、膨胀、硬化、褪色或异常气味，应立即停止使用。
  - step_id: care.pvc.storage
    instructions:
      en: Store clean and fully dry in a cool, dry, shaded place, without long-term pressure, sharp contact, dark fabric contact, or direct contact with other soft plastics and elastomers.
      zh: 清洁并完全干燥后存放在阴凉、干燥、避光处，避免长期受压、尖锐物接触、深色织物接触，以及与其他软塑料和弹性材料直接接触。
  - step_id: care.pvc.recheck
    instructions:
      en: Inspect for hardening, cracking, tackiness, swelling, dye transfer, odor, and coating changes. Contact support before applying any stain remover, solvent, oil, heat, or repair product.
      zh: 检查是否出现硬化、开裂、粘腻、膨胀、染色、异味或涂层变化。使用任何去渍剂、溶剂、油剂、热源或修复产品前先联系人工客服。

# PVC and soft-vinyl care baseline

This is the company-wide approved PVC and soft-vinyl baseline. Formulations vary, so product-specific
supplier limits take precedence. Stop when the cleaner, water exposure, lubricant, coating, or
storage-contact restriction is unknown.
