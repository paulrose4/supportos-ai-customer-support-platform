approved_steps:
  - step_id: care.silicone.identify-and-inspect
    instructions:
      en: Confirm the exact model, silicone components, coatings, makeup, hair installation, and any TPE or electronic parts. Stop for tears, mold, loose bonded areas, exposed structure, or unknown materials.
      zh: 确认准确型号、硅胶部件、表面涂层、彩妆、植发方式，以及是否存在 TPE 或电子部件。发现撕裂、霉菌、粘接松动、结构外露或未知材料时停止处理。
  - step_id: care.silicone.power-and-accessories
    instructions:
      en: Power off the product, disconnect only user-removable power parts, and remove clothing, wigs, and accessories that may bleed color or trap moisture.
      zh: 关闭产品电源，仅断开用户可拆卸的供电部件，并移除可能染色或积聚水分的衣物、假发和附件。
  - step_id: care.silicone.protect-detail-areas
    instructions:
      en: Protect makeup, painted areas, eyelashes, eyes, implanted hair, adhesives, seams, and electrical interfaces from rubbing and uncontrolled water exposure.
      zh: 避免彩妆、涂色区域、睫毛、眼睛、植发、粘接处、接缝和电子接口受到摩擦或未经控制的进水。
  - step_id: care.silicone.surface-clean
    instructions:
      en: Use a soft non-abrasive cloth with lukewarm water and only the mild cleaner approved for this model. Clean gently without hard scrubbing, stretching, boiling, steaming, or unapproved soaking.
      zh: 使用柔软无研磨性的布、温水以及该型号明确允许的温和清洁剂轻柔清洁，不用力摩擦、不拉伸、不蒸煮，也不进行未经批准的浸泡。
  - step_id: care.silicone.cavity-clean
    instructions:
      en: Clean internal cavities only with the model-approved irrigation tool and method. Keep water away from heating, sensors, motors, connectors, and other non-waterproof components.
      zh: 内部空腔只能使用该型号认可的冲洗工具和方法清洁，并避免水进入加热、传感器、电机、接口和其他非防水部件。
  - step_id: care.silicone.remove-residue
    instructions:
      en: Remove approved-cleaner residue using the water exposure allowed for the exact model, without forcing water into seams, bonded areas, or internal structures.
      zh: 按准确型号允许的进水范围清除清洁剂残留，不强行让水进入接缝、粘接区域或内部结构。
  - step_id: care.silicone.dry
    instructions:
      en: Blot with a clean lint-free cloth and air-dry completely in shade with ventilation. Do not use direct heat, sunlight, a hot-air dryer, or reconnect power while moisture remains.
      zh: 使用干净无绒软布轻压吸水，并在阴凉通风处彻底自然干燥。仍有水分时不得使用直接热源、阳光、热风，也不得重新通电。
  - step_id: care.silicone.powder-rule
    instructions:
      en: Do not apply TPE renewal powder, oil, or surface treatment unless the silicone product supplier explicitly requires and approves it for the exact coating and finish.
      zh: 除非硅胶产品供应商针对该型号涂层和表面明确要求并批准，否则不要使用 TPE 护理粉、油剂或其他表面处理产品。
  - step_id: care.silicone.lubricant
    instructions:
      en: Use only a supplier-approved water-based lubricant. Do not assume silicone-based or oil-based products are compatible with silicone coatings, bonded areas, or accessories.
      zh: 仅使用供应商批准的水性润滑剂。不得假设硅基或油基产品一定兼容硅胶涂层、粘接区域或附件。
  - step_id: care.silicone.storage
    instructions:
      en: Store the clean, fully dry product in a cool, dry, shaded location, support the torso and load areas, keep joints neutral, and isolate the surface from dark fabrics, sharp objects, and other elastomers.
      zh: 将清洁且完全干燥的产品存放在阴凉、干燥、避光处，支撑躯干和受力区域，关节保持自然位置，并与深色织物、尖锐物和其他弹性材料隔离。
  - step_id: care.silicone.recheck
    instructions:
      en: Inspect stored products for dye transfer, coating changes, tackiness, loose makeup, adhesive failure, moisture, and joint stress. Contact support before stain removal or repair.
      zh: 检查存放产品是否出现染色、涂层变化、粘腻、彩妆松脱、粘接失效、潮湿和关节受力。去渍或维修前先联系人工客服。

# Silicone care baseline

This is the company-wide approved silicone baseline. Product-specific instructions and supplier
limits take precedence when they are more restrictive. Stop and contact support when the coating,
cleaner, water-resistance boundary, electronics, or structural condition is unknown.
