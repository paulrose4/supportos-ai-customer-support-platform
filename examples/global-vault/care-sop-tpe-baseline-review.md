approved_steps:
  - step_id: care.tpe.identify-and-inspect
    instructions:
      en: Confirm the exact model and TPE body material. Stop and contact support if there is a tear, crack, mold, exposed skeleton, electrical feature, or unknown coating.
      zh: 确认准确型号和 TPE 身体材质。发现撕裂、开裂、霉菌、骨架外露、电子功能或未知涂层时停止处理并联系人工客服。
  - step_id: care.tpe.power-and-accessories
    instructions:
      en: Power off the product, disconnect only user-removable power parts, and remove clothing, wigs, and accessories that may bleed color or be damaged by moisture.
      zh: 关闭产品电源，仅断开用户可拆卸的供电部件，并移除可能染色或受潮损坏的衣物、假发和附件。
  - step_id: care.tpe.surface-clean
    instructions:
      en: Use a soft non-abrasive cloth with lukewarm water and only the mild cleaner approved for this model. Wipe gently without stretching, scrubbing, or soaking the TPE.
      zh: 使用柔软无研磨性的布、温水以及该型号明确允许的温和清洁剂轻柔擦拭，不拉伸、不用力摩擦，也不浸泡 TPE。
  - step_id: care.tpe.cavity-clean
    instructions:
      en: Clean internal cavities only with the model-approved irrigation tool and method. Do not use high pressure, hard brushes, sharp tools, or force the opening.
      zh: 内部空腔只能使用该型号认可的冲洗工具和方法清洁，不使用高压水流、硬刷、尖锐工具，也不强行扩大开口。
  - step_id: care.tpe.remove-residue
    instructions:
      en: Remove all approved-cleaner residue with the minimum water exposure allowed by the supplier, keeping water away from joints, connectors, and non-waterproof components.
      zh: 按供应商允许的最低进水范围清除清洁剂残留，并避免水进入关节、接口和非防水部件。
  - step_id: care.tpe.dry
    instructions:
      en: Blot moisture with a clean lint-free cloth and air-dry fully in a shaded, ventilated area. Do not use a hair dryer, heater, sunlight, or other direct heat.
      zh: 使用干净无绒软布轻压吸水，并在阴凉通风处彻底自然干燥。不得使用吹风机、暖气、阳光暴晒或其他直接热源。
  - step_id: care.tpe.verify-dryness
    instructions:
      en: Confirm that folds, seams, joints, and internal cavities are completely dry before powdering, dressing, closing, storing, or reconnecting power.
      zh: 上粉、穿衣、封闭、收纳或重新接通电源前，确认褶皱、接缝、关节和内部空腔已经完全干燥。
  - step_id: care.tpe.powder-if-approved
    instructions:
      en: Apply only the powder type and amount approved by the product supplier, and only after complete drying. Keep powder away from eyes, makeup, hair roots, adhesives, and electrical interfaces.
      zh: 仅在完全干燥后使用产品供应商批准的粉剂种类和用量，并避开眼睛、彩妆、发根、粘接区域和电子接口。
  - step_id: care.tpe.lubricant
    instructions:
      en: Use only a supplier-approved water-based lubricant. Stop if the surface becomes sticky, swollen, discolored, softened, or develops an unusual odor.
      zh: 仅使用供应商批准的水性润滑剂。若表面出现粘腻、膨胀、褪色、软化或异常气味，应立即停止使用。
  - step_id: care.tpe.storage
    instructions:
      en: Store the clean, fully dry product in a cool, dry, shaded place, supported at the torso and major load areas, with joints in a neutral position and no prolonged pressure from dark fabrics or other soft plastics.
      zh: 将清洁且完全干燥的产品存放在阴凉、干燥、避光处，支撑躯干和主要受力区域，关节保持自然位置，并避免深色织物或其他软塑料长期压迫表面。
  - step_id: care.tpe.recheck
    instructions:
      en: During long-term storage, inspect for pressure marks, dye transfer, tackiness, oil migration, odor, moisture, and joint stress. Contact support before attempting treatment or repair.
      zh: 长期存放期间检查压痕、染色、粘腻、渗油、异味、潮湿和关节受力情况，进行去渍、处理或维修前先联系人工客服。

# TPE care baseline

This is the company-wide approved TPE baseline. Product-specific instructions and supplier limits
take precedence when they are more restrictive. Stop and contact support when the material,
coating, cleaner, electronics, or structural condition is unknown.
