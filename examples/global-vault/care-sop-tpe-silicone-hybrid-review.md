  - step_id: care.hybrid.identify-sections
    instructions:
      en: Confirm the exact model, the silicone head or silicone sections, the TPE body sections, the neck connection, coatings, makeup, hair installation, and any electronic features.
      zh: 确认准确型号、硅胶头或硅胶部件、TPE 身体部件、颈部连接结构、涂层、彩妆、植发方式以及所有电子功能。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.inspect-and-stop
    instructions:
      en: Stop and contact support if there is a tear, crack, mold, loose neck connection, adhesive failure, exposed skeleton, electrical feature, or uncertainty about which material covers an area.
      zh: 发现撕裂、开裂、霉菌、颈部连接松动、粘接失效、骨架外露、电子功能，或无法确认某一区域材质时停止处理并联系人工客服。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.power-and-accessories
    instructions:
      en: Power off the product, disconnect only user-removable power parts, and remove clothing, wigs, and accessories that may bleed color or trap moisture.
      zh: 关闭产品电源，仅断开用户可拆卸的供电部件，并移除可能染色或积聚水分的衣物、假发和附件。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.separate-care-zones
    instructions:
      en: Treat the silicone head and TPE body as separate care zones. Do not transfer TPE powder, oil, cleaner residue, or tools to silicone makeup, hair, eyes, adhesives, or the neck connector.
      zh: 将硅胶头和 TPE 身体作为两个独立护理区域，不让 TPE 粉剂、油剂、清洁剂残留或护理工具接触硅胶彩妆、头发、眼睛、粘接处和颈部接口。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.clean-silicone-section
    instructions:
      en: Clean silicone sections with a soft non-abrasive cloth, lukewarm water, and only the model-approved mild cleaner, protecting makeup, implanted hair, eyes, seams, adhesives, and connectors.
      zh: 使用柔软无研磨性的布、温水以及该型号允许的温和清洁剂清洁硅胶部件，并保护彩妆、植发、眼睛、接缝、粘接处和接口。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.clean-tpe-section
    instructions:
      en: Clean TPE sections with a separate soft non-abrasive cloth, lukewarm water, and only the model-approved mild cleaner. Do not stretch, scrub, soak, or expose the body to direct heat.
      zh: 使用另一块柔软无研磨性的布、温水以及该型号允许的温和清洁剂清洁 TPE 部件，不拉伸、不用力摩擦、不浸泡，也不让身体接触直接热源。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.cavity-clean
    instructions:
      en: Clean cavities only with the exact model's approved tool and method. Keep water and cleaner away from the neck connector, adhesives, electronics, and non-waterproof structures.
      zh: 内部空腔只能使用准确型号认可的工具和方法清洁，并避免水和清洁剂进入颈部接口、粘接区域、电子部件和非防水结构。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.dry-separately
    instructions:
      en: Blot the silicone and TPE sections with separate clean lint-free cloths and air-dry completely in shade with ventilation. Do not use sunlight, hot air, heaters, or reconnect parts while moisture remains.
      zh: 使用不同的干净无绒软布分别轻压吸干硅胶和 TPE 部件，并在阴凉通风处彻底自然干燥。仍有水分时不得暴晒、使用热风或暖气，也不得重新连接部件。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.powder-tpe-only
    instructions:
      en: If the supplier requires powder for the TPE body, apply only the approved powder and amount after full drying, keeping it completely away from the silicone head, makeup, hair roots, eyes, adhesives, and connector.
      zh: 如果供应商要求为 TPE 身体上粉，只能在完全干燥后使用批准的粉剂和用量，并确保粉剂不接触硅胶头、彩妆、发根、眼睛、粘接处和接口。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.lubricant
    instructions:
      en: Use only a water-based lubricant approved for both the TPE and silicone components. Stop for tackiness, swelling, discoloration, softening, coating change, or unusual odor.
      zh: 仅使用同时获得 TPE 和硅胶部件兼容确认的水性润滑剂。出现粘腻、膨胀、褪色、软化、涂层变化或异常气味时立即停止使用。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.storage
    instructions:
      en: Store clean and fully dry, support the torso and neck according to the model manual, keep joints neutral, and isolate both materials from dark fabrics, sharp objects, and other soft plastics or elastomers.
      zh: 清洁并完全干燥后存放，按照型号说明支撑躯干和颈部，关节保持自然位置，并让两种材料都与深色织物、尖锐物以及其他软塑料或弹性材料隔离。
  - step_id: care.hybrid.recheck
    instructions:
      en: Inspect the neck connection, adhesive lines, makeup, dye transfer, TPE tackiness or oil migration, moisture, and joint stress. Contact support before stain treatment, adhesive work, or repair.
      zh: 检查颈部连接、粘接线、彩妆、染色、TPE 粘腻或渗油、潮湿和关节受力情况。去渍、处理粘接或维修前先联系人工客服。

# Hybrid-material care baseline

This is the company-wide approved silicone-head/TPE-body baseline. Treat the head and body as
different materials, follow product-specific supplier limits, and stop when the connection,
coating, cleaner, water-resistance boundary, or structural condition is unknown.
