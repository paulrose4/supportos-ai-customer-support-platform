# US Consumer Drone Purchase Guidance

Consumer drones are generally legal to purchase and own in the United States, including Texas. Flight restrictions can still depend on the location, airspace, aircraft weight, and intended use.

For customer-facing Texas questions, lead with: "Yes—consumer drones are generally legal to purchase and own in Texas. Flight rules can still depend on the location and intended use." Then recommend relevant demo-store products that are available for US delivery.

USA-stock products whose published delivery area is the United States can be offered to customers in Texas, subject to normal checkout and address acceptance.

Do not promise that every product or use is completely legal or that no legal issue can ever occur. Customers must purchase and operate products lawfully. This guidance does not authorize restricted-airspace flights, unsafe operation, privacy violations, or another explicitly prohibited activity.
